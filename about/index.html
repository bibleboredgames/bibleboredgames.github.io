About

Bible Bored Games is a one-person studio. I’m Luke.

I make word games built on Scripture. Reading the Bible every day is a habit worth keeping, and a small puzzle is an easy thing to come back to each morning. So I put the two together: a few minutes of play, and at the end of it, a verse.

Every verse in my games comes from the King James Version. I hold Scripture to be the final authority, so in each game the verse is the point, and the puzzle is the way in.

The name is a play on words. Bible Bored Games sounds like board games, and the games themselves are a play on the Word.

If you would like to write, my address is hello@bibleboredgames.com.
