Bible Enigma

Coming soon to the App Store

Each day, a verse of the King James Bible is written in numbers. Work out the letters, one square at a time, until you can read it.

There are two ways to play. In Verse Cipher, every letter is a number, and the same number always means the same letter. In Missing Words, you fill the blanks from a word bank. Everyone gets the same verse on the same day.

The game’s own site has screenshots and the full guide to playing.
