theWord

Daily Bible Puzzle · On iPhone and Android

Each day, a five-letter word from Scripture. You have six tries to find it. When you do, the verse it comes from opens.

App Store Google Play

How to play

Type a five-letter word and press Enter. Each letter changes color to tell you how close you are. Use what you learn on the next try. Everyone gets the same word on the same day, and a new one starts at midnight, your phone’s time.

  • DThe right letter in the right place
  • LIn the word, but in another place
  • GNot in the word

Also in the game

  • A short devotional on each day’s verse.
  • A streak that counts the days in a row you solve it.
  • One hint per puzzle, if you want it. It shows one letter.
  • Play Past Words, for more practice.
  • 365 words, a full year, every verse from the King James Version.
  • No account and no sign-up. It plays offline.

theWord is free. A short ad may play before a hint, and you get the hint either way.
